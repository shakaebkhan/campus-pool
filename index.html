How it works

  1. Choose a ride

    Select your area and direction. Every ride shows its route, timing and seats left.

  2. Pick your stop

    The fare depends on how far your stop is from campus. See co-riders on the map.

  3. Verify with OTP

    Enter your mobile number, type the 6-digit OTP, and your seat is confirmed.
